Common questions

How do I delete my account?

Open the app, go to Profile, then Settings, and choose Delete Account. This removes your profile, posts, listings, messages, and photos. Deletion is permanent and can't be undone. If you can't get into the app, email us from the address on your account and we'll handle it.

How do I report or block someone?

Every profile, post, listing, and comment has a Report option, and profiles also have Block. Blocking hides you from that player and them from you, and removes any friendship between you. Reports go to a moderator, not to the person you reported.

Is the marketplace safe?

Every listing is reviewed by a person before other players can see it. We don't process payments or shipping — buyers and sellers arrange that themselves, so treat it like any local sale: meet somewhere public, inspect before you pay, and report anything that looks wrong.

Who can message me?

Only players you're connected to — friends, people in your LAN parties, and people going to the same events. Anyone else has to send a request you can accept or decline first.

Is LAN Parents for kids?

No. LAN Parents is built for parents, and accounts are for adults. Kids are who we're all playing with at home, not who's on the app.
